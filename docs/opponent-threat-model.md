# UO-Monte — tournament opponent threat model
**November 21, 2026 | Coordinator analysis | Strategy/testing hypotheses, not evidence of future opponents' private code.**

**Goal:** Identify ways competent, rule-compliant heads-up No-Limit Texas Hold'em bots might beat UO-Monte. Guard against them by matchup-diverse **paired-seat, same-deal** evaluation and original, legal strategy code. Avoid overreacting to any one toy opponent or matchup. Human teammates control all deployment.

- Committed V3.4 `feature/strategy` `a5ea561ff5b70d5a17533579bfc6557b2979622b`. Its unsolved preflop scalar score/thresholds and generic free-check flop/turn `equity >= 0.72` policy are confirmed by source code. The `_opponent_history`, `_river_aggression`, `_wide_betting_evidence`, and `_river_bet_responses` functions partially adapt using `match_action_log` but do not retain full-match sufficient statistics beyond its sliding 200 entries.
- Claude TASK-004 independent test of **two artificial, nonbluffing opponents** found weak flop/turn float calls, poor bluff-catching against value-only river bets, and over-calling strong check-raises. These are useful hypotheses, **not representative tournament forecasts**.
- V3.5, V3.6, V3.6.1, V3.7 produced no reliable, repeatable large improvement on fresh seeds. Keep committed V3.4 protected until a stronger verified candidate exists.

## Required adversarial benchmark design
1. **Pair by deal and swapped seat.** Tournament ranks pair wins/draws/losses, not only average individual-game wins.
2. **Keep opponent diversity.** Compare Caller, Aggressive, Tight, Mixed and Floorfix (report fixed vs rising blinds separately) plus independent Overfolder/Trapper **and at least one bluff-capable/adaptive opponent** before claiming improved defense. If new opponents aren't available, state the gap rather than overclaim.
3. **Use a matchup × street × size matrix** with opponent bets in categories small/medium/pot/overbet/all-in, preflop 3-bet/4-bet, free-check and check-raise, plus early/mid/late match, stack depths **3.5/7.5/12/25/100BB**, ante onset and blind-transition boundaries. Only include parameter combinations supported by official runner; otherwise build isolated valid state/unit tests, not fabricated runner flags.
4. **Observe action-change frequency and conditional profitability cautiously.** Lost chips after a call do **not** automatically imply the call was negative expected value; conditioning on showdown creates selection bias. Report cumulative bankroll/match wins, pair W/D/L, call/raise/fold opportunities, stack-off frequency, latency and errors.
5. **Protect holdouts.** Keep independently chosen fresh seed blocks; once holdout is inspected for tuning it becomes development data. Never use sealed seeds **1001–1200** for iteration.
6. **No automatic adoption.** A change that crushes value-only Trapper but loses to Aggressive/Mixed may decrease overall tournament performance. Promote only by combined fresh-match evidence and human approval.

## Tournament rules and intellectual-property gate
- Bot Python **3.10**; allowed imports: stdlib, `eval7`, `numpy`, `scipy`, `treys`, `scikit-learn`; **2 seconds/decision**, **0.5 CPU**, **768 MB**.
- **No network, live LLMs, forbidden filesystem writes, subprocesses/threads, reflection, hidden opponent cards/code, collusion or edits to `pokercore/`**. No memory across matches unless built into a pre-approved static model before the event.
